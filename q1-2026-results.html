Strong Q1 performance for Individual Annuities and International Wealth, partially offset by lower sales in Bulk Annuities, Home Finance and Group Protection. Q1 2025 was boosted by the record quarter we saw in International Wealth.

Sales exclude Canada Life Asset Management.

Base earnings are higher year-on-year compared to Q1 2025 (+£4.2m), primarily driven by stronger trading activity and higher insurance earnings through growth in the underlying business.

This is an unaudited non-GAAP financial measure.

Strong case flow and positive momentum continued from Q4 2025. A strong January reflected focused action to recover the new business servicing position, resulting in pipeline being cleared and strengthening issued business.

Slower completions in January reflecting lower application volumes in Q4 2025, however volumes improved following rate changes. Sales are expected to increase in Q2, supported by further rate changes and the launch of Advantage.

Although volumes were broadly consistent with Q1 2025, deal timing is volatile and the whole market reflected a slower start to the year. There is currently a healthy pipeline for higher Q2 and Q3 volumes.

Strong performance highlights continued demand for our international products, although not at the record levels seen in Q1 2025. A strong pipeline is in place heading into Q2.

The low new business volumes written in January have been partially offset by above plan results in February and March. Increased quote activity in Q1 has led to a strong sales pipeline for Q2.
